100% Offline • Zero Data Collection

Privacy Policy

Spelling Challenge — Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Spelling Challenge ("the App") is a fully offline educational spelling quiz game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal user data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, Spelling Challenge:

  • Operates 100% offline and does not require an internet connection.
  • Does not require user accounts, login, or registration.
  • Does not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.
  • Does not include advertisements, ad networks, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Does not connect to any external API, backend server, or cloud database.

2. Local On-Device Storage

The App saves only one piece of local game progress directly on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Your highest score achieved in the game.

This value never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Children's Privacy

Because the App works completely offline and collects zero personal information from any user, it is safe for players of all ages, including children.

4. Device Permissions

The App does not request internet access or any sensitive device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates to the App change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before releasing the update.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the support email address listed on the Spelling Challenge Google Play Store page.
